# Warm beige limestone

Original procedural base-color PNG, generated by scripts/generate-limestone.py
with deterministic seed 912. No reference photograph or external asset used.
The 1024px square represents 1.2 × 1.2 metres: staggered 60 × 30cm blocks,
subtle warm variation and fine grain, without baked lighting.

Applied to the living-room interior faces of Wall with openings 10 (west,
x=1, z=10.3..16), 11 (south, z=16), and 12 (east, x=9).
World-aligned UVs preserve physical scale across window openings.
Exterior faces, wall edges, trim, openings and collisions remain unchanged.
sRGB color map, roughness 0.94, metalness 0; no deep relief is implied.
